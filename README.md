# Awasunu Orginale
This is the basic original version of Awasunu. I eventually also merged it with Obelisk.

## Bequest De Cendres And Lhacurimosa
This is an evolution of the Bequest De Cendres Engine where Awasunu and Nuerisupu spun off into becoming their own DSLs.

The games mostly serve historical purposes, and aren't reflective of current codebase developments.

## Post Game Research
This later evolved beyond its original domain. I primary use this for research probabilistic pattern that determine most likely player patterns.

## For New Developments
One experiment is in how you can have a MC give instructions to a character to perform actions. The other is designed to mirror Grandia-like mechanics, evolving the concept with Priority turn order based on probability, rather than random behaviours. Much of this went onto inform the decision making for Awasunu.
