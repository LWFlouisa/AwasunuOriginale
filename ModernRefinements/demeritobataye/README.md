# Demeritobataye
Demerits of battle screens. The logic inverse of Victory Screens in RPGs.

## Context
In Grandia, the MCs portraits are showen beside their current stats, which are given level up based reward incentives.

Here the concept is adapted where ( depending on your scaling mode ) will get negative stat boost or stat reduction upon recieving certain demerits in battle. The hardest mode is Machine Learning based mode that's akin to a comet shower or running the gauntlet / being keelhauled through the bottom of a ship.

Historically Keelhauling was reserved for people whom commit crimes on the high seas like Mutiny.
