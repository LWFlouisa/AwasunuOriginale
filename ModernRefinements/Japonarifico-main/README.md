# Japonarifico
A prototype for a DSL for my game engine focusing on proper honorific placement.

## Dystopian Command Structure
The context of this commaand structure assumes a Franco-Japanese War context in my science fiction setting, and therefore are not intended as a good thing, but representative of how much society has left to go before men and women are truly equal. Neuter honorifics are a special case, as they can take on a women's form, and yet have all of the abilities that any male unit can have in campaign structures.

In context, this specific DSL evolved out of a mental excercise for managing how people change the context around a word rather than the word directly in order to rapidly alter its meaning. Here the "toxic mushroom" being used as an example to indicate that which contains a literal meaning, and meaning where someone refers to someone as being a "toxic mushroom."

## Masculine Gender Honorifics
Masculine honorifics are the main honorific system, with the implicit assumption that such units default to masculine oriented units of command. Women only very recently became able to join resistance paramilitaries in this science fiction setting, with the expectation that they use feminine honorifics. Therefore part of a mens units training is in detecting infiltrators  that are female in gender, and therefore require different processing.

## Feminine Gender Honorifics
Feminine honorifics largely match the masculine system, except that feminine honorific units are their own system of command organizationally. This has implications down the road such as the kind of missions they might be sent on to get information about enemy militaries.

This has sense been baked into general Ahuzacos culture by having otherwise anarchist groups still using said systems of command when getting information about fascists, and other types of male dominated units.                                           

## Neuter Gender Honorifics
Nuetral honorifcs are used primarily to refer to machines like AI systems and androids, and noteable for being the only category where baby AI systems can speak and obey authority when ones honorific is Kun.
